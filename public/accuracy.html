How accurate is Snowline?

Every morning Snowline saves its own snow forecast. After each storm it compares that forecast with what actually fell, using government snow gauges and Mt. Bachelor's own snow report. Here's the season so far.

How it's graded

Same rules for every spot

The forecast: the one Snowline showed the morning before each snow day, saved automatically and never edited afterward.

What fell: the nearest SNOTEL snow gauge (an automated U.S. government station that measures new snow and the water in it), and for Mt. Bachelor, the resort's daily 24-hour snow report.

A storm counts as close when Snowline was within 2″, or within 25% on storms over 8″. Measured depth runs a bit low because new snow settles, so the water amount is used when picking the closest weather model.

Snowline is free forever, with no ads and no account. Snow data from USDA NRCS SNOTEL and Mt. Bachelor; weather models via Open-Meteo.
